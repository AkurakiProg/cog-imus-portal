Our Ministry Framework

The W.O.R.D.A. Pillars

Rooted in Scripture, our church life and ministry departments are thoughtfully structured across five foundational pillars of faith.

01 Worship

Worship

Exalting God in spirit and truth through vibrant praise, adoration, and music ministry.

02 Outreach

Outreach

Sharing the love and gospel of Christ with Imus and beyond through compassionate mission.

03 Relationship

Relationship

Cultivating genuine family bonds, fellowship, and mutual encouragement across all ages.

04 Discipleship

Discipleship

Equipping believers to grow into Christ-like maturity through sound biblical teaching and mentorship.

05 Action

Action

Translating faith into selfless service, faithful stewardship, and servant leadership in action.
